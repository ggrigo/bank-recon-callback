bank-recon — Terms of use

Scope

bank-recon is a personal Account Information Service (AIS) integration used by the application owner to consolidate that owner's own SME bank accounts. The application is not offered as a service to third parties. No public registration or sign-up is provided.

Use

Only the application owner authorizes the application, only against their own bank accounts, under explicit PSD2 consent granted at the bank level. Strong customer authentication (SCA) is performed by the bank's own systems; this callback page does not collect or transmit any credentials.

Limitation

The application is provided as a personal data-plumbing tool. It carries no warranty and the application owner is solely responsible for any consequences of its use against their own accounts. The application owner accepts the Enable Banking API terms of service at auth.enablebanking.com/terms.

The application owner may update or discontinue the application at any time without notice.
