bank-recon — Privacy notice

This page is the OAuth redirect target of bank-recon, a personal Account Information Service (AIS) integration owned and operated by the application owner (Georgios Grigoriadis) for the sole purpose of consolidating that owner's own SME bank accounts. The application is not offered as a service to anyone else.

What data is processed

Bank account balances and transaction history are retrieved by the application owner's local machine from Account Servicing Payment Service Providers (banks) via the Enable Banking API, under explicit PSD2 consent given by the same individual whose accounts they are.

Where data goes

All retrieved data is written to local files on the application owner's own computer. No data is transmitted to any third party, no analytics or telemetry of any kind are collected, no remote logs are maintained, and the callback page you are reading runs entirely client-side without server logging.

What this callback page does

When your bank redirects you back from PSD2 SCA, the URL carries code and state query parameters. This page reads them via JavaScript, renders them so you can copy the URL into the local script running on your machine, and does nothing else. Those values do not leave your browser tab.

Retention

The application owner is the data controller, processor, and sole data subject. Local snapshots are retained on the owner's machine as long as they are useful and can be deleted at any time by the owner.
